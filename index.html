ABOUT SHIVA ENTERPRISES

Built on craftsmanship, experience and attention to detail.

Shiva Enterprises is a construction and civil works firm based in Taksal Chowk, Rekabganj, Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh. We undertake construction and finishing work across Ayodhya, Lucknow and nearby cities.

Our team has experience in specialized stone fitting work, including work associated with Ram Mandir, as well as red sandstone and Rajasthani marble fitting.

We also undertake concrete, tile fitting and other related construction and civil works.

We have also carried out minor construction work at the Allahabad High Court.
